# Blockparty Product Roadmap
### The daily ritual app for your neighborhood. Local IG feed meets Nextdoor, anchored by games.

**What the product is:** A place you check once a day because it's fun, and leave knowing your neighborhood a little better. Games are the front door. The feed, brief, and community layers are why you stay. Nextdoor owns local anxiety; Blockparty owns local pride.

---

## The four product pillars

### 1. Games - the daily ritual
Not one Wordle. A rotating suite of games that can only be played by someone who lives there, all sharing streaks, leaderboards, and share cards.

- **The Daily Word** (live): local Wordle, post-solve "why this word" blurb
- **The Mini**: 5x5 daily crossword, every clue local ("Sugar ___", "___ Chica")
- **Spotted**: photo geo-game. A zoomed or cropped photo of somewhere in town, guess the spot, map pin scoring. UGC photo supply feeds it
- **Listed**: guess the sale price of a real home that just sold in the neighborhood. Price Is Right mechanics. This is the real estate module disguised as a game, and the most shareable thing on the site ("I was off by $400k")
- **Then/Now**: historical photo, guess the year or the location. Historical societies love to supply these
- **Pick'em**: weekly predictions on local high school games. Season-long standings
- **The Bracket**: seasonal 16-seed tournaments voted daily. Best burrito in HB, best sunset spot, best happy hour. Two weeks of daily voting, massive share bait, and the winners list becomes evergreen content

### 2. The Brief - need-to-know
The 90-second read attached to the games, not a separate news site.

- **The Daily Five**: five one-line items. Restaurant openings/closings, local sports results, crime blotter digest, development and project updates, notable real estate
- **Weekend Guide**: Thursday drop, what's happening
- **Alerts**: road closures, surf/beach conditions, school calendar oddities
- Crime rule: dry factual digest, never a discussion thread. The anti-Nextdoor design decision

### 3. The Feed - local pride, photo-first
The Instagram layer. Scrollable cards, good news weighted.

- Sunset/beach/food/dog photos from locals, lightweight posting
- **First Look**: photos and quick reviews from just-opened spots
- **Shoutouts**: positive-only tags for people and businesses
- Game results cards from friends and neighbors mixed in
- Business profiles: hours, story, what locals order. Claimed by owners later
- Moderation defaults: photos before text, no anonymous posting, no crime or politics threads

### 4. Community - identity and rivalry
- **Neighborhood leaderboards**: daily, weekly, all-time across all games
- **Profiles and badges**: streaks plus local identity badges (Pier Regular, Bolsa Birder, Main Street Mayor, earned through play and posting)
- **The Weekly Recap**: "HB averaged 3.8 guesses. 212 of you got FREETH in two."
- **Town Rivalries**: HB vs Newport vs Costa Mesa weekly game matchups, aggregate town scores. Cross-town trash talk is the multi-city growth engine hiding inside a feature
- **Word/photo submissions**: locals supply puzzle content, credited by name. Community becomes the content pipeline

## Release plan

### v1 - The Ritual (now)
The Daily Word, share cards, streaks, blurbs. One page. Ship analytics and the share URL. Custom domain deferred for the pilot.

### v1.5 - The Suite starts (month 1-2)
- The Mini crossword (weekends first)
- The Daily Five on the results screen
- Email: daily brief + streak protection
- Named leaderboard once player density supports it

### v2 - The Games Hub (month 2-4)
- Home becomes a hub: today's games as cards, brief below
- Add Listed and Spotted (the two most shareable, both feed future content modules)
- Accounts (email login), profiles, badges, stats that survive devices
- Weekly Recap email
- First Bracket tournament as an event

### v3 - The Feed (month 4-7)
- Photo-first UGC feed with posting
- First Look and Shoutouts card types
- Business pages (unclaimed, data-seeded; claiming comes with monetization)
- Then/Now and Pick'em join the suite
- Push notifications: "Today's word is up" is the only default-on notification

### v4 - The Town Layer (month 7-10)
- Town Rivalries live with a second city
- Full brief modules as browsable sections: Openings map, Scores, Projects tracker ("what's being built on Beach Blvd"), Real estate recent sales
- Events calendar with promotion slots
- Comments, tightly scoped: on games and feed posts only, never on crime or news items

### v5 - The Platform (month 10+)
- City template: word pipeline, game configs, brief sources, seeding playbook packaged for launch-in-a-week
- Local sponsorships: sponsored puzzle ("Today's Mini presented by..."), business spotlights, real estate placements on Listed, event promotion. Sponsor the shareable moment, never banner ads
- Creator/contributor roles: local photographers, historians, sports parents get bylines and status
- API/data layer for permits, listings, scores automation across cities

---

## What Blockparty is not
- Not a news site with games on it. Games are the front door, always
- Not Nextdoor: no crime threads, no lost-cat-turned-argument, no politics
- Not Patch: no newsroom cost structure. Content comes from data, games, and locals
- Not a national app with a location filter. Every city instance feels handmade for that town

## The moat
Habit plus identity. Anyone can clone a local Wordle. What compounds is the streak someone won't break, the badge that says Pier Regular, the leaderboard with their neighbors' names, and a content pipeline the community feeds itself.
